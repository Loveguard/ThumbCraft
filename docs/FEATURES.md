# Everything ThumbCraft can do

## Feature reference

| Area                   | What works                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Biome scenes**       | Pick a dimension (Overworld / Nether / End) and any biome from your game version, optionally a seed and an area size, and generate.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| **Accuracy**           | Terrain comes from Mojang's own world generator. Block models, textures, biome colours and the font are read from **your** Minecraft install (details below).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **World import**       | Cut an area out of any of your singleplayer saves (Minecraft 1.18+).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Builds**             | Quick props in one click (nether portal, treasure pile, ore vein wall, TNT stack, sky island, grand watchtower, and a beacon lit with the game's beam or unlit), plus a **tree brush**: click the ground to plant varied oak, birch, spruce or dark oak trees. Search every structure in your Minecraft version by tag (village houses for each biome, igloos, shipwrecks, ruined portals, bastions, ancient cities, trail ruins…), straight from the game's structure files. Import `.schem` (WorldEdit/Sponge), `.litematic` and structure-block `.nbt` files downloaded from schematic sites. |
| **Mobs**               | Every entity model the game defines (mobs, plus chests, boats, signs, armor stands…), exported from the game's own model code, with a texture-variant picker and per-limb posing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Particles**          | Every particle texture from the jar, as pixel-perfect sprites with optional in-game-style tinting (the Game particles section of the FX tab).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Effects**            | Colour looks as real 3D LUTs (six clearly different grades, Fuji film, Blockbuster, Moonlight, Noir, Toxic and Neon, with an intensity slider, or import any `.cube` LUT), manual exposure, contrast, saturation, warmth/tint and split toning. Focus blur driven by draggable on-screen focus points (several subjects can be sharp at once). Lens warp, speed burst, anamorphic streaks, chromatic aberration, vignette, film grain and a CRT TV look (over the scene only, or over titles too). Bloom and god rays with a movable sun. **Keep titles clean** draws always-on-top layers after the effects. Everything is included in exports.               |
| **FX**                 | Placeable smoke, ground fog, fire, explosions, embers, magic aura, light beams, lightning strikes (with adjustable thickness) and shine (twinkling star glints for loot, diamonds and armour), plus 3D rain and snow.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| **Players**            | Type a username: the skin is fetched from Mojang, with slim (Alex) arms detected, the outer layer, legacy 64×32 skin conversion and pose presets. Every joint is adjustable, including **elbows and knees**: click a limb of the selected player for rotate handles, use the joint sliders, or drag hands and feet directly (IK). Steve and Alex are built in.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Armor & items**      | Any armor set from your game version (leather dyeable) on players, bending with the pose, and any item or block held in either hand, placed the way the game holds it. Shields too, held like in game (raised across the chest in the Blocking pose). Four custom **outfits** worn instead of armour: King (crown, cloak, sceptre), Astronaut, Soldier and Wizard (hat, robe, staff); the Players tab adds a character in one.                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Lights**             | Point lights and spotlights in any colour, one-click looks (rim light, torch glow, neon, window and leaf shadows), spotlight patterns or your own image, sun/ambient controls and a night look. Torches, lava, glowstone and other glowing blocks light up their surroundings like in-game.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Reflections**        | Mirrors, and reflective lakes and oceans.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| **Media**              | Import transparent PNG/WebP/JPEG/GIF images, MP4/WebM videos, and 3D models (.glb, .gltf, .obj with textures).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| **Text**               | Outlined thumbnail-style title text (gradients, double outlines, glow, custom fonts), 3D voxel text from Minecraft's font, carved stone titles in any colour, and front-on Hardcore titles where every O becomes the hardcore heart.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| **Interface**          | Labelled icon rail with one asset panel at a time (click the open icon to fold it away); Look, Mobs and Builds are organised in colour-coded collapsible sections. A calm, single-surface layout: the top bar holds only the project menu, Preview and Export (size and file type in its menu); every editing tool (transform, undo/redo, camera angles, saved view, frame all, grid, snap, rulers) lives in one dock under the canvas; messages float in briefly instead of a status bar; Hide panels (\\) clears both side panels away. Selection toolbar, collapsible inspector, keyboard shortcuts card in the project menu, undo/redo (Ctrl+Z also steps back through camera moves). |
| **Editing**            | Hold **Shift** while moving (or turn on Snap) to snap onto surfaces and block/model corners. New objects appear where you're looking (players and mobs stand on the ground, text floats, builds snap to the grid) and face the camera. Move/rotate/scale gizmo (W/E/R), duplicate, a layers list, **Bring forward / Send backward** (moves toward or away from the camera), Layer up/down and "Always on top" for overlays.                                                                                                                                                                      |
| **Layering**           | Any object can be **always on top** (drawn over terrain and trees in front of it) and use **flat lighting** (its own colours, evenly bright, untouched by the scene's lights and shadows).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Layers**             | Rows tinted by kind (green terrain, blue players, red mobs, yellow lights, pink text, purple media, orange effects); duplicate names are numbered.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Selection & groups** | Ctrl/Shift+click or Shift+drag a box to select several objects, then move, turn or resize them together around their centre (one undo step). **Ctrl+G** groups them so one click picks the whole group; groups show as folders in the Layers list.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| **Rulers**             | A top-bar toggle (Shift+R) adds pixel rulers along the frame, centre and rule-of-thirds guides, and a readout of where the selection sits; the centre guides light up green when the selection is centred.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| **Rendering**          | _Vanilla_ mode copies the game's look: the same face shading, smooth-lighting AO and 5×5 biome blend. _Cinematic_ adds real lights and shadows, _Soft_ a warm shader-pack look, and _Cartoon_ the bright cel-shaded style of Minecraft's key art. Any 3D object can be drawn always on top and with flat lighting. Adjustable FOV (defaults to 70 like the game).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Projects**           | Save/open `.bfproj` files (Ctrl+S / Ctrl+O). Everything a scene needs, including terrain and your imported media, is packed inside, so a project opens identically on another PC. Autosave every few seconds, recent projects on the start screen, and recovery after closing without saving.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| **Sky**                | Vanilla biome sky, physical skies with clouds (clear, golden hour, sunset, overcast), night with the game's moon, the End sky, a solid colour, or your own HDRI / panorama (.hdr, .exr, .jpg, .png), which can also light the scene in Cinematic mode.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| **Tabs**               | Several projects open at once, one tab each, each with its own undo history and camera. New project and Open use a new tab (or the current one while it's still empty); **Duplicate as new tab** makes an unsaved variant. Tabs can be reordered by dragging; closing one with unsaved changes asks first, and every tab keeps a recovery copy. Ctrl+T new tab, Ctrl+Tab / Ctrl+Shift+Tab switch.                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| **Thumbnail kit**      | 2D stickers drawn flat over the frame: arrow, curved arrow, circle, cross, tick, burst and box, in any colour with outline, glow and mirror. Line thickness for every shape but the burst; tail length for arrows and width for circles and boxes. Drag, resize, turn and stretch them in the viewport (Shift snaps the angle to 15°). Split images put two open tabs side by side with a straight, torn-paper or lightning divider, adjustable lean, position and colour.                                                                                                                       |
| **YouTube preview**    | A mock YouTube page with your thumbnail (and every other open tab) rendered into the home feed, search results, the 168-pixel "up next" list and a phone, in dark or light mode, with your title, channel name and video length.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Export**             | JPG (auto-fitted under YouTube's 2 MB limit) or PNG at 1280×720, 1080p, 4K or 1080×1920 (Shorts), or several sizes at once. The viewport is locked to the export aspect ratio, so the preview matches the export. Helpers like the grid and gizmo are hidden.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
